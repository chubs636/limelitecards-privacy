LimeliteCards Analytics Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

LimeliteCards Analytics is a privately operated, self-hosted analytics application used to review eBay seller activity and business performance.

Information accessed

When authorized by the account owner, LimeliteCards Analytics may access eBay seller information required to provide analytics and reporting, including order details, sold-item information, listing identifiers, seller-defined SKUs, fulfillment information, and related seller performance data made available through eBay APIs.

How information is used

Information is used only to generate private analytics, dashboards, filters, summaries, and business reports for the authorized seller account.

Data storage

Data retrieved from eBay is stored on the account owner's self-hosted server/NAS. LimeliteCards Analytics does not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties.

Credentials and authorization

The application uses eBay OAuth authorization. eBay usernames and passwords are not stored by LimeliteCards Analytics. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored privately by the account owner and are used only to access authorized eBay API data.

Data retention and deletion

The account owner controls the local data store and may delete synchronized eBay data or revoke eBay authorization at any time.

Third-party services

LimeliteCards Analytics communicates with eBay APIs. Use of eBay services is also subject to eBay's own terms and privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or the LimeliteCards Analytics application may be directed to the application owner through the associated LimeliteCards eBay seller account.
